2. Information We Collect

Depending on how the app is used, BLC Schedule Tracker may collect or process the following information:

  • Access codes entered to use the app.
  • User role information, such as student, viewer, or administrator.
  • Class cycle and schedule information displayed in the app.
  • Schedule details created, edited, imported, or managed by authorized users.
  • PDF or schedule content imported into the app, if the import feature is used.
  • Push notification permission status, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens, and notification topic subscriptions.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate the app, such as app, browser, device, network, and diagnostic information handled by Firebase services.
  • Advertising-related information processed by Google Mobile Ads, such as device identifiers where available and permitted, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information.
  • Advertising consent choices recorded through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide access to BLC Schedule Tracker features.
  • Show the correct schedule and class cycle to users.
  • Allow authorized users to manage calendar and schedule information.
  • Keep users logged in when they choose the keep-login option.
  • Register devices for schedule notifications and deliver role- or cycle-related notifications.
  • Request and display personalized or non-personalized advertising when permitted by the user's consent choices and applicable law.
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the app.

4. Keep-Login Storage

If a user chooses the keep-login option, the app may store login information locally on the user's device or browser so the user does not need to re-enter the access code each time. Users can remove this local login information by logging out, clearing browser or app data, or uninstalling the app.

5. Firebase Services and Push Notifications

BLC Schedule Tracker uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Realtime Database and Firebase Cloud Messaging, to store and sync schedule-related data, register devices for notifications, and deliver schedule alerts. An FCM registration token identifies an app installation for notification delivery; it is not used by the app as a user's name. Firebase may process technical and service data as part of providing these services. Google's privacy practices are described in Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Advertising and Consent

The app uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers where available and permitted, IP address, device and network information, ad impressions, interactions, and diagnostic information to provide, measure, prevent fraud in, and improve advertising services.

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for advertising consent before requesting ads. Depending on the user's location, choices, and device settings, Google may provide personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads, or no ad may be available. When Google indicates that a privacy options entry point is required, users can open Settings > Ad Privacy Choices in the app to review or change their advertising consent choices.

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers, including Google Firebase, Google AdMob, and their authorized service providers and advertising partners, as needed to operate the app, deliver notifications, provide and measure advertising, prevent fraud and abuse, comply with legal obligations, protect users, or enforce applicable terms and policies.

8. Data Retention

Schedule and access-related information is retained for as long as needed to provide the app, manage class schedule records, maintain security, resolve issues, or comply with applicable obligations. Local keep-login data remains on the user's device until the user logs out, clears local data, or removes the app. FCM registration tokens and notification subscriptions may be retained while notifications are enabled and are removed or invalidated when notifications are disabled, the user logs out, the app is uninstalled, or the token is refreshed, subject to service operation and backup retention.
