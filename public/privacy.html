This Privacy Policy explains how BLC Calendar collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the BLC Calendar application and related services.

This policy identifies the app operator and the contact email for privacy questions, support, and data deletion requests.

1. App Information

App name: BLC Calendar
iOS Bundle ID: dev.pages.blccalendar

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how the app is used, BLC Calendar may collect or process the following information:

  • Access codes entered to use the app.
  • User role information, such as student, viewer, or administrator.
  • Class cycle and schedule information displayed in the app.
  • Schedule details created, edited, imported, or managed by authorized users.
  • PDF or schedule content imported into the app, if the import feature is used.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate the app, such as app, browser, device, or network information handled by Firebase services.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide access to BLC Calendar features.
  • Show the correct schedule and class cycle to users.
  • Allow authorized users to manage calendar and schedule information.
  • Keep users logged in when they choose the keep-login option.
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the app.

4. Keep-Login Storage

If a user chooses the keep-login option, the app may store login information locally on the user's device or browser so the user does not need to re-enter the access code each time. Users can remove this local login information by logging out, clearing browser or app data, or uninstalling the app.

5. Firebase and Third-Party Services

BLC Calendar uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Realtime Database, to store and sync schedule-related data. Firebase may process technical and service data as part of providing these services. Google's privacy practices are described in Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for third-party advertising. Information may be processed by service providers, such as Firebase, only as needed to operate and maintain the app, comply with legal obligations, protect users, or enforce applicable terms and policies.

7. Data Retention

Schedule and access-related information is retained for as long as needed to provide the app, manage class schedule records, maintain security, resolve issues, or comply with applicable obligations. Local keep-login data remains on the user's device until the user logs out, clears local data, or removes the app.

8. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of information associated with their use of BLC Calendar by contacting us at doesntmatter0828@gmail.com. We may need enough information to verify the request and identify the relevant records.

9. Children's Privacy

BLC Calendar is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review and delete the information if required.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised last updated date.
